# Singularity Labs — Future Ideas

This document contains ideas that may be implemented in future versions.

Ideas in this document are NOT part of the current development scope unless explicitly moved into `docs/roadmap.md`.

---

# Forecasting

Potential future direction:

Build a quantitative forecasting layer on top of Singularity Labs' clinical and biomedical intelligence.

Potential concepts:

* probability of clinical outcomes
* probability of trial success
* probability of progression to the next development stage
* probabilistic drug-development forecasts
* company pipeline forecasts
* evidence-weighted forecasting

---

# Prediction-Market Concept
